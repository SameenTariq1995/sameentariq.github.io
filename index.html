About Me

I am a development economist and PhD candidate at the University of East Anglia, specializing in the economics of education. My research examines how behavioural interventions and training programs can influence decision-making, resilience, and learning outcomes using field and laboratory experiments.

I have substantial research experience in both Pakistan and the United Kingdom. Before starting my PhD, I worked as a Short-Term Consultant with the World Bank (SARCE and Sustainable Development teams), a Senior Research Assistant at the Centre for Economic Research in Pakistan (CERP), and a Teaching Fellow at the Information Technology University (ITU). I currently serve as an Associate Teacher in Economics at the University of East Anglia.
